**[The Goetheanum](https://goetheanum.ch/en) is located in Dornach near Basel in Switzerland. The Goetheanum serves as the center of the worldwide Anthroposophical Society and the seat of the School of Spiritual Science.**

With approximately 150,000 visitors annually, nearly 200 conferences of varying sizes, and a diverse offering of cultural events, the Goetheanum is dedicated to serving the spirit of the time with a thoughtful, human-oriented approach that can respond to the manifold and pressing inner and outer questions of the day.
