Dear Alums, Faculty, and Friends of CfA,

As we approach the end of 2017, we have much to be grateful for: our amazing students, vibrant programs, dedicated faculty and staff, and our many, many alums.

Working together, our impact on the world is growing steadily at a time when progressive education, civility, and healing are needed more than ever. Thank you for being part of our growing movement for social change!

Our programs here in New Hampshire have flourished since the opening of Waldorf teacher education at Antioch University New England in 1982.

Thanks to CfA, our high school teacher education program has been instrumental in providing teachers for many new high schools around the world.

Foundation Studies have been offered in over two-dozen communities across the continent, and our Renewal Courses attract as many as 250 people per week. It seems these efforts serve a real need.

Next week––on “Giving Tuesday”, November 28th––we will need your support.

On that day, thanks to a nationwide match offered by Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, we have a chance to give our scholarship programs a significant boost. Beginning at 8:00 a.m., please go to [https://www.facebook.com/donate/709697219227553/](https://www.facebook.com/donate/709697219227553/) for the Center for Anthroposophy and help us sustain our work in 2018.

With gratitude,

Torin M. Finser,

*President,

CfA Board of Trustees*

### Help us to spread the work of the Center for Anthroposophy

**We’re asking for your help on November 28th, 2017**

Dear friends and supporters,

We are very excited to announce our participation this year in Giving Tuesday, a global day of giving to support the organizations that do important work across the world. We are also excited to announce that we are eligible for a matching grant from the Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation. The Gates foundation has partnered with Facebook to create a matching grant for organizations raising money through Facebook’s charitable giving tools.

To compete for these matching funds, we really need your help. If you support CFA and have a Facebook account, [please visit our Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/Renewal3/) where you will find detailed instructions on how to set up a Facebook Fundraiser on behalf of CFA and invite your friends to support us on Giving Tuesday.

If you are not comfortable creating a Facebook fundraiser, you can still [donate on Tuesday via Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/donate/709697219227553/10207979654128354/) at the link below, which will also still help CFA compete for the matching grant money. If you are not on Facebook, please be assured that your continued support for our organization is deeply appreciated and although you cannot help us compete for matching funds, you can help support CFA on Giving Tuesday by donating through our [website at the link below](https://centerforanthroposophy.org/make-a-donation/).

With sincere gratitude for all of your past, current, and future support,

The Center for Anthroposophy Board and Staff
