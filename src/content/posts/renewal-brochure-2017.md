Dear Friends,

We are excited to announce our [2017 Renewal Courses](https://centerforanthroposophy.org/programs/renewal-courses/), with an international line-up of stellar instructors from around the world. We hope you will join us again this year on our beautiful High Mowing Hill for a time of personal rejuvenation and social renewal through anthroposophical study, artistic immersion, good food, and fun.

We look forward to seeing you again and hope that you will share our Renewal e-Brochure and Renewal Poster with your friends, colleagues, and social networks. Thank you for helping us spread the word and helping Renewal to prosper into the future!
