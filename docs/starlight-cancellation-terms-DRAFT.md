# Starlight Rays 2026–2027 — cancellation terms (DRAFT, not policy)

Drafted 2026-09-02 by Claude for Sage as a starting point for David Barham and Milan Daler.
Nothing here is CfA policy until they say so. Modeled on the terms already published on the
Renewal Courses, Explorations Online, Mentor Training, Leadership Development, and Building
Bridges pages (30 days' notice, $75 fee, no refund inside the window), adapted for a
12-seminar series with single-session tickets and a five-payment plan.

Once approved, this text goes on a single `/policies/cancellation` page alongside the five
existing program paragraphs, and the registration checkboxes and welcome emails link to it.

---

## Full series (individual registration, $420)

Cancel by email to office@centerforanthroposophy.org before the first seminar on September 5,
2026, and we'll refund your registration less a $75 cancellation fee.

Once the series has begun there are no refunds, since recordings and resources for every
seminar stay available to you through the season. You can transfer your registration to a
colleague at any time by emailing the office.

## Five-payment plan

The payment plan is a commitment to the full $420 registration, paid as $84 today and $84 a
month for the following four months. The terms above apply: cancel before September 5 and
we'll refund what you've paid less $75 and cancel the remaining payments; after that, the
remaining payments continue as scheduled.

If a payment can't be collected, we'll email you to update your card. Access to the seminars
and recordings pauses if a payment is more than 30 days late and resumes as soon as the plan
is current. You can pay the remaining balance early at any time by contacting the office.

## Institution registration ($1,220, up to 20 participants)

Same terms as the full series. The school can change who is on its roster at any time, so a
participant who leaves mid-season can be replaced rather than refunded.

## Single seminars ($19) and the three-seminar bundle ($44)

These include the live seminar and its recording, so they aren't refundable. Before the
seminar takes place you can transfer a ticket to another person by emailing the office.

## If CfA cancels a seminar

We'll reschedule it or provide a recorded alternative. If a seminar is cancelled outright and
not replaced, series registrants receive a refund of that seminar's share ($35), and single
seminar ticket holders receive a full refund for it.
