When: Sun, Jan 14, 2018 2:00 – 3:00pm

Where: Golden Valley Orchard School 6550 Filbert Avenue, Orangevale, CA 95662 Room 9

Contact: [co@goldenvalleycharter.org](mailto:co@goldenvalleycharter.org) 916-597-1478

At the invitation of Golden Valley Charter Schools, Torin Finser, Chair of the Education Dept at Antioch University New England, will present options for Waldorf teacher training through the Center for Anthroposophy and Antioch.

Featured in the presentation will be an exciting new CfA option, the Building Bridges Program, which could be offered on campus at Golden Valley next school year!

Courses designed for practicing teachers, will take place one weekend a month. Participants may be eligible to graduate from Antioch with a certificate of Waldorf Teacher Training.

[For more information, please visit the Building Bridges Program](https://centerforanthroposophy.org/building-bridges-waldorf-teacher-training/)
