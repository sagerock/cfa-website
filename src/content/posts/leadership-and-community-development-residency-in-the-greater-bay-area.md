Dear Colleagues,

We are delighted to announce our** first ever** leadership and community development residency in the greater bay area of California! The Yuba River school will be hosting us **September 17-20, 2026** with a possible second residency in 2027. The virtual sessions begin **May 9 **with a one-hour welcome and orientation. The complete schedule can be found on our website:

[https://centerforanthroposophy.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/WLCD2627Yuba-River-Link.pdf](https://centerforanthroposophy.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/WLCD2627Yuba-River-Link.pdf)

Please use the attached flyer to reach out to your faculty, staff and board so they can let you know of their interest. Three or more from one school allows for a 20% discount to the rates on the flyer. This is a one-time event to support your administrators, staff, board, and present (and emerging) faculty leadership in their roles with a targeted professional development program designed just for them!

Thank you for helping us recruit from your school.

Yours

*Torin*

PS If you have program questions, please do not hesitate to reach out to Karen Atkinson, our program coordinator: [karen@centerforanthroposophy.org](mailto:karen@centerforanthroposophy.org)
