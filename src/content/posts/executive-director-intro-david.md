#### Winter 2024

####

David Barham

*By David Barham*

For many, these are scary times. An unstable and unpredictable political and economic situation, fires and floods, an unknown and unknowable future calling to us. Rudolf Steiner tells us, “*Above all, we need to cultivate courage and fearlessness in the inmost depths of our thought life itself.*” (from *How to Know Higher Worlds: A Modern Path of Initiation*)

Here at Center for Anthroposophy, we are deepening our commitment to providing teacher education and professional development programs that provide guidance for those seeking to help bring healing and education to a scared world.

This newsletter that you are reading, Center & Periphery, is a report from our various fields of endeavor: Who we are, what we have been doing and how we are seeking to help. Please enjoy these articles about some of our many programs, and our wonderful alumni.

Drop a line and let us know your thoughts about our work and tell us how you like Center & Periphery.

Yours,

**David Barham**
