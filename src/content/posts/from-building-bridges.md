# Message from the Executive Director

*By Torin M Finser*

In February we will complete our BB program at the Ocean Charter School in LA and start a new cluster at the Mountain Phoenix Community School in Wheat Ridge – Denver area in Colorado. Although participants of any given group consist mostly of faculty, staff, and community members in one particular school, individuals within driving distance of a participating site are welcome to register as well. [https://centerforanthroposophy.org/programs/building-bridges-to-waldorf-teacher-training-2/](https://centerforanthroposophy.org/programs/building-bridges-to-waldorf-teacher-training-2/)

For the 2026-2027 school year, we are considering a Waldorf Leadership & Community Development or Building Bridges site in the Sacramento area of California. If you or your school is interested, please contact [Deborah@centerforanthroposophy.org](mailto:Deborah@centerforanthroposophy.org).

Thank you to all of our previous hosts and participants who made it possible to build bridges to Waldorf teacher education in Anchorage, AK, Atlantic Beach, FL, Baltimore, MD, Golden Valley and Grass Valley, CA, and Denver, Colorado!

Torin M Finser

Spring/Summer 2022
