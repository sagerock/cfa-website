**Karin Meadows**, alumna of the Waldorf Program at Antioch University New England and currently Business Manager at the Yuba River Public Waldorf Charter School in Grass Valley, CA, was voted onto CfA’s Board of Trustees at its Spring 2023 meeting.

Karin’s lifelong study of humankind has led her from religion and philosophy to education – specifically, how educational institutions serve the public good based on a developmental model of education. Beyond her Waldorf credentials, Karin’s background in business and technology has already proved invaluable in helping to revise the operations of CfA’s administrative office. In addition, she has taken on the tracking of courses and credits for participants in the Center’s Kairos Institute. We are delighted to welcome Karin into the circle of our trustees.
