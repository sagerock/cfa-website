[https://centerforanthroposophy.org/programs/waldorf-administration-and-leadership-development-program/](https://centerforanthroposophy.org/programs/waldorf-administration-and-leadership-development-program/)

**Building Bridges**: WLD in the Denver area will be followed by a Building Bridges cohort starting in February 2026:

[https://centerforanthroposophy.org/programs/building-bridges-to-waldorf-teacher-training-2/](https://centerforanthroposophy.org/programs/building-bridges-to-waldorf-teacher-training-2/)
