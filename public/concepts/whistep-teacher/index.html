Why now

“They carry a biography shaped by a level of global turbulence few cohorts have faced so young.”

As I think about Adriel and his generation, I find myself reflecting deeply on the reality modern adolescents face, and on what Waldorf education is called to become for them.

In WHiSTEP and in our online seminar series, Starlight Rays in Darkened Times, we are wrestling with the essential questions. What must Waldorf education become to meet both the times and the young human beings before us? What must each teacher transform in themselves?

Every sincere question seems to point toward a single answer: become ever more human. Learn to read the riddle of each individual and commit our fullest strength to meeting them where they are.

It is nothing less than a sacred calling.

From David’s letter in Center & Periphery, Winter 2025–2026.
