# Geophysiology Enhanced Through Eurythmy Gestures

Summer is a sacred pause in the rhythm of the year—a time to rest, reflect, and prepare. Renewal Courses offer a week-long professional development experience designed to replenish your spirit, deepen your relationship with Waldorf education and anthroposophy, and connect you with a vibrant community.

Open to educators, administrators, parents, artists, and seekers from both independent and public Waldorf schools, Renewal Courses are offered **in-person **and** online** over **two independent weeks each summer**. Whether you’re preparing for Grades 1–8, Teaching Combined Classes, or exploring the full Waldorf Journey from Grades 1–12, each course provides:

- **Inspiring instruction** grounded in child development and anthroposophy

- **Resources and tools** to support your year ahead

- A **30-hour professional development certificate**

- **Community gatherings** with daily singing, decolonial dialogues, and shared purpose

- **Evening lectures, peer reflection, and wellness offerings** for whole-person nourishment

In- person participants will enjoy a rich array of self-care opportunities—your chance to truly rest, replenish, and nourish your work-life balance.

Choose from:

- Holistic Custom Facial

- Healing Massage

- Sound Healing

- Forest Bathing Explorations

- Guided Bird Walks

- Artistic Engagement for the Soul

Wherever you are on your path, Renewal is here to support, inspire, and welcome you.

Join us and return to school renewed**. **

***Karen, Director of Renewal Courses**
