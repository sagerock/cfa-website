In partnership with The Center for Anthroposophy and Antioch University, Yuba River Charter School is excited to host the Building Bridges Waldorf Teacher training at our new campus during the 2019-2020 school year!

- One year program 2019-2020

- One weekend a month

- Located at the new YRCS campus in Grass Valley, CA

- Counts as the Foundations Year AND one summer of Waldorf teacher training

- Deposits will secure your place in this rare local opportunity

[Visit this link for more information](https://centerforanthroposophy.org/programs/building-bridges-waldorf-teacher-training/) or contact Karin Meadows at karin@yubariverschool.org
