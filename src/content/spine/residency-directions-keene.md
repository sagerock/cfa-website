### Residencies Location

[Gathering Waters Charter School](https://www.gatheringwaterscharter.org/), High School Campus at 31 Washington St, Keene, NH 03431.

### How to get there

By air:

- Closest Airport is [Bradley International](https://bradleyairport.com/) by Hartford, CT (ground transport to Keene might be a challenge

- [Boston Logan International](https://www.massport.com/logan-airport) + [Boston Express Bus to Nashua Exit 8](https://www.bostonexpressbus.com/stop/nashua-nh-exit-8/) + S&K Taxi: 603-882-5155 or (Uber/Lyft)

- [Manchester Boston Regional Airport](https://www.flymanchester.com/) and [ground transportation](https://www.flymanchester.com/ground-transportation/taxis-shuttles-limos/) or Uber/Lyft

By train: purchase [Amtrak tickets to Brattleboro, VT](https://www.amtrak.com/stations/bra)

By car: [Google Maps directions](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/98+S+Lincoln+St,+Keene,+NH+03431/@42.9336468,-72.2689781,17z/data=!4m9!4m8!1m0!1m5!1m1!1s0x89e1747684d6479b:0x9ecad206dd231293!2m2!1d-72.2664032!2d42.9336429!3e0?entry=ttu)
