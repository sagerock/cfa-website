The children and adolescents in our Consciousness Soul times need to integrate into the challenging world around them which assaults their developmental stages. We see the struggles in their behaviors and diverse learning needs. You may also be struggling mightily at times as teachers, administrators, and parents, searching for new approaches with practical insights and solutions.

We are offering an advanced short training that will allow you to ask your questions and process your issues. We have invited James Dyson, Kim John Payne, and Tonya Stoddard, three stellar individuals with extensive background in the current issues we are facing today. They will give daily presentations, facilitate discussion groups, and answer your questions. Spiritual and medical insights, pedagogical practices, work with family systems, and anthroposophic psychology, will enkindle your courage to bring healing forces into your classroom, school, home, or practice.

There will be daily singing with Cale Brandley, and healing workshops in movement with Cezary Ciaglo, Maria Ver Eecke and Jan Lyndes (participants choose one class). Those interested in our new Animal Assisted Therapy track will join Brian Jacques.

We are delighted that Adam Blanning will offer a live Zoom Tuesday, July 8, 7:00pm evening lecture:

Embracing and Overcoming Developmental Displacements- How to Love the Children Being Born Today Even More

Thursday evening, July 11, 7:00pm we have invited Glenn Williamson and Laurie Portocarrero with the production of *Fire in the Temple.*

Both evening events are open to the public.
