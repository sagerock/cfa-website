Our alumni directory is up and running, and it has been great to see many of you signing on, making profiles, and connecting with other alumni. Some of you have reported diﬀiculties accessing the site – please reach out to alums@cfa.org and we can help you get connected. We are looking forward to creating more groups for people to connect to and share news and resources.

We hope that you have been enjoying the alumni feature articles in Center and Periphery.

If you have news to share or if you would like to recommend an alum for us to reach out to for an article, please contact us.

The annual Abbot Hill Alumni Summer Gathering will take place on July 3 in the Big Room at High Mowing. Our theme is *Rooted: Uniting Our Present Purpose to our Anthroposophical **Foundation*. Join us for a community gathering and discussion of our theme, followed by cake and our traditional dancing with music by Tattoo!

RSVP to [alums@cfa.org](mailto:alums@cfa.org).

The Abbot Hill Alumni group sends congratulations to all of our future members who will be graduating from their programs in July!

We wish you a joyful summer on “the hill,” and can’t wait to see where your futures will take you.
