The leadership team of the Alumni Association extends a warm and enthusiastic invitation to a Homecoming Party, scheduled to take place at High Mowing School on Tuesday, July 5, 2022, starting at 7:00 p.m. All graduates of the Waldorf teacher training programs of Antioch University and Center for Anthroposophy are invited to this festive gathering. Join with old friends and classmates to share stories, reminisce about the past, and to dream about the future!

In addition, because this event will also commemorate the 40th anniversary of Waldorf teacher education at Antioch University, there will be opportunity to hear stories from some of the “old-timers” who were instrumental in establishing this program. There will even be a cake with 40 candles.

Later in the evening, a live band composed of some of our very own grads will bring Abbot Hill to life with their toe-tapping music.

We hope to see many of you there. If you are planning to come, please RSVP to Jenna John at [jenna@centerforanthroposophy.org](mailto:jenna@centerforanthroposophy.org).
